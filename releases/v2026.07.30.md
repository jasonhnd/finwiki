## 日本語

### 公開範囲

- Issue #239 の released-main `dc5d3584` baseline にある `fintech/` 65 source paths / 162 table-related warnings を bounded scope とする。
- 内訳は 158 `table_without_provenance` と、`global-stablecoin-regulatory-five-pole-matrix.md` にある 4 `table_row_without_marker` findings である。
- 65 source entries、対応する ja / en 130 mirrors、三言語 release / operating surfaces、root index / AI discovery surfaces を同じ release unit で同期する。
- `.opinions/**`、`docs/**`、人手判断を待つ Issue #193、後続 Issue #240–#244、非 owned paths は変更しない。

### 主要変更

- GENIUS Act の現行記録を S.1582 / Public Law 119-27 に揃え、S.394 を introduced predecessor として分離した。OCC の proposal、conditional approval、final charter、Federal Reserve account access を別々の status として扱い、未公布の実施細則や自動的な決済網アクセスを確定事実から除外した。
- Digital pound、digital euro、Drex、e-CNY、e₹、Sand Dollar、eNaira、JAM-DEX、Project Agorá / mBridge について、consultation、design、pilot、MVP、handover、issuance decision、production を公式資料の基準日に合わせた。比較不能な wallet、user、transaction、adoption 指標を単一 ranking にしない。
- MiCA interim register、日本の電子決済手段制度、香港の発行者制度、東南アジア・中南米の当局資料を再確認し、licence、registration、distribution、reserve、redemption、AML/CFT、means-of-payment restriction を法域ごとに分離した。未確認の市場シェア、地域 dominance、制度導入確率は削除した。
- Franklin Templeton、Paxos、Tether、Circle / Coinbase、tokenized MMF、主要 stablecoin issuer の product terms、reserve / collateral、direct redemption、assurance、eligible holder を公式開示へ揃えた。volatile market cap、固定順位、将来 revenue split、保証利回りを current fact として扱わない。
- Cross-chain、embedded wallet、wholesale settlement、issuer / distributor、sovereign-currency boundary の表では、technical architecture、trust assumption、legal counterparty、commercial scenario を分けた。ja / en mirrors は日付、法的 status、数値、否定・条件表現、URL、wikilink、table / list shape を source correction と同じ意味へ同期する。

### 既知の注意点

- provenance audit は read-only / advisory であり、162 warnings の解消は corpus 全体または各 entry の全 prose を一括保証しない。実証範囲は owned tables と修正に必要な直近 context である。
- 規制、pilot、licence、issuer register、reserve、AUM、market cap、network availability は基準日後に変化し得る。proposal、計画、conditional approval、operational launch を同じ status として一般化しない。
- Official product page や attestation は、そのページが明示する product、measurement date、eligible customer、assurance scope だけを支える。市場全体の順位、全 holder の直接償還権、政府保証、将来利回りを自動的には証明しない。

## English

### Release Scope

- Use the 162 table-related warnings across 65 `fintech/` source paths in Issue #239's released-main `dc5d3584` baseline as the bounded scope.
- The baseline comprises 158 `table_without_provenance` findings plus four `table_row_without_marker` findings in `global-stablecoin-regulatory-five-pole-matrix.md`.

- Leave `.opinions/**`, `docs/**`, human-review Issue #193, downstream Issues #240–#244, and non-owned paths unchanged.

### Major Changes

- Aligned the current GENIUS Act record to S.1582 / Public Law 119-27 and separated S.394 as an introduced predecessor. OCC proposals, conditional approvals, final charters, and Federal Reserve account access are treated as distinct statuses; unpublished implementation rules and automatic payment-rail access are no longer stated as facts.
- Aligned the digital pound, digital euro, Drex, e-CNY, e₹, Sand Dollar, eNaira, JAM-DEX, Project Agorá, and mBridge to official evidence dates for consultation, design, pilot, MVP, handover, issuance decision, and production. Incomparable wallet, user, transaction, and adoption measures are not collapsed into one ranking.
- Rechecked the MiCA interim register, Japan's electronic-payment-instrument framework, Hong Kong's issuer regime, and authority materials across Southeast Asia and Latin America. Licence, registration, distribution, reserves, redemption, AML/CFT, and means-of-payment restrictions are separated by jurisdiction; unsupported market share, regional dominance, and adoption probabilities were removed.
- Aligned Franklin Templeton, Paxos, Tether, Circle / Coinbase, tokenized MMFs, and major stablecoin issuers to official product terms and disclosures for reserves or collateral, direct redemption, assurance, and eligible holders. Volatile market caps, fixed ranks, future revenue splits, and guaranteed yields are not presented as current facts.
- Cross-chain, embedded-wallet, wholesale-settlement, issuer / distributor, and sovereign-currency tables now separate technical architecture, trust assumptions, legal counterparties, and commercial scenarios. The ja/en mirrors synchronize dates, legal status, figures, negation and conditions, URLs, wikilinks, and table / list shape with the corrected sources.

### Known Notes

- The provenance audit remains read-only and advisory. Eliminating 162 warnings is not a blanket guarantee for the corpus or every paragraph in each entry; the evidenced scope is the owned tables and the immediate context required for correction.
- Regulations, pilots, licences, issuer registers, reserves, AUM, market caps, and network availability can change after the evidence date. Proposals, plans, conditional approvals, and operational launches must not be generalized as one status.
- An official product page or attestation supports only the product, measurement date, eligible customer, and assurance scope it states. It does not automatically establish a market-wide rank, direct redemption rights for every holder, a government guarantee, or future yield.
