Second release of 2026-05-25 JST. Right after wave 5 (v2026.05.25) landed, launched wave 6 with 10 parallel agents and shipped 25 new comparison matrices in a single batch. An AI / LLM can now answer a "comparison question" via one matrix retrieval rather than synthesizing across many entity pages.
