# Japan credit-card receivable ABS — evidence-bound reading

## TL;DR

No stable public transaction document was located that supports the previous JPY 400–700 billion market estimate, six-issuer ranking, master-trust series count, 3–9-month issuance cadence, receivable balances, default ranges or 期間延長 timetable. Those values are removed. A card-receivable transaction may be revolving or may use a trust, but its mechanics must come from the named documents.

## 1. Issuer and program verification

[JSDA's securitization-market survey](https://www.jsda.or.jp/en/statistics/securitization-market/) is a market entry point; the following table states the program evidence still required.

| Field | Required public evidence |
|---|---|
| Legal issuer / trust | Offering, trust or rating document |
| Originator and servicer | Transaction documents |
| Program / series history | Dated issuer or trustee list |
| Issuance amount | Named series result or JSDA table with definition |
| Parent / affiliation | Current company disclosure |

## 2. Stand-alone and multi-series structures

[JCR's general structured-finance methodology](https://www.jcr.co.jp/en/pdf/dm28/General_Methodology20210802.pdf) supports reviewing the following structural fields.

| Field | Required named-deal evidence |
|---|---|
| Asset pool | Dedicated or shared pool definition |
| Series claim | Allocation of collections and losses |
| Replenishment | Eligibility, substitution and end conditions |
| Cross-series effects | Priority, dilution and shared-trigger provisions |
| Trustee / accounts | Legal holder and account controls |

Do not call a structure a “master trust” merely because receivables revolve.

## 3. Receivable composition

[The Installment Sales Act](https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/336AC0000000159) supplies a statutory framework; the actual pool still requires the following disclosure.

| Receivable field | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Purchase / installment / revolving / cash advance | Eligibility definition and balance at cutoff |
| Yield and fees | Contractual components and denominator |
| Delinquency / charge-off | Definitions, aging and recoveries |
| Payment rate | Numerator, denominator and observation period |
| Concentration | Account, channel, geography and vintage fields disclosed |

Statutory categories do not establish pool APRs or default rates.

## 4. Extension and amortization

[JCR's general methodology](https://www.jcr.co.jp/en/pdf/dm28/General_Methodology20210802.pdf) supports the following document-reading checklist.

| Control | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Revolving period | Contractual start and end |
| Scheduled amortization | Class-specific payment formula |
| Extension option | Holder, conditions, notice and maximum period |
| Early amortization | Exact metric, threshold and consequence |
| Legal maturity | Named series terms |

No standard 期間延長 timetable or threshold is asserted.

## 5. Enhancement and rating

[JCR's methodology library](https://www.jcr.co.jp/en/rrinfo/meth_sf/) supports identifying the following fields, not standard percentages.

| Claim | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Subordination / reserve / OC | Named closing structure |
| Excess spread | Contractual formula and trapping |
| Rating | Named class, date and criteria version |
| Performance | Dated trustee / servicer report |
| Spread | Named pricing result and benchmark |

## 6. Deferred claims

Market size, issuer rank, buyer base, series cadence, balance ranges, default rates and extension periods remain deferred until supported by a public named transaction or defined official statistical table.
