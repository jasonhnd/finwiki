# Aircraft leasing and financing — verified Japan-linked platforms

## TL;DR

The cited company pages verify several Japan-linked aviation-leasing businesses and one dated SMFG acquisition disclosure. They do not support a fleet ranking, standard JOL/JOLCO leverage, tenor or investor economics, a market-leader hierarchy, or a generic aircraft-ABS structure. This page therefore records only verified company relationships and a named-transaction evidence checklist.

## 2. Financing-structure evidence

[Mitsubishi HC Capital's aviation page](https://www.mitsubishi-hc-capital.com/english/corporate/business/aviation.html) does not establish a universal financing stack; use the following named-transaction checklist.

| Field | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Owner / lessor / lessee | Executed lease and ownership documents |
| Debt | Facility amount, lender, security, amortization and covenants |
| Equity / investor interest | Offering and partnership / contribution documents |
| Tax treatment | Current law plus investor and transaction facts |
| Purchase / residual value | Appraisal, maintenance status and sale assumptions |
| Currency / hedge | Lease currency, debt currency and hedge contracts |

## 3. Product distinctions

[ORIX's aircraft and ships page](https://www.orix.co.jp/grp/en/about/businesses/aircraft_ships/) illustrates that company activities can span services; the following table prevents product conflation.

| Product | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|
| Operating lease | Named aircraft, owner, lessee and lease |
| JOL / JOLCO | Named investor offering, legal form, debt and tax analysis |
| Secured aircraft loan | Facility and security documents |
| Aircraft ABS | Named issuer, asset pool, notes, waterfall and rating report |
| Sale-and-leaseback | Purchase and lease documents |

## 4. Deferred claims

Fleet ranks, market shares, 50–80% leverage, 8–12-year terms, investor yields, tax benefits and “leader” labels remain deferred until supported by a public named transaction or defined dated population.
