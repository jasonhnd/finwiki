# Seven & i finance deep dive

## Scope

This page reflects the post-2025 structure: Seven Bank is no longer a consolidated subsidiary of Seven & i Holdings, and Seven Bank itself owns the card, nanaco, and payment-service subsidiaries described below. It also records ITOCHU's later investment and the completed deconsolidation of YORK Holdings. It does not preserve the earlier, stale “Seven & i owns about 46% and directly owns all finance arms” model.

## 2025–2026 ownership reset

Seven Bank repurchased shares from three Seven & i subsidiaries on 2025-06-24. This reduced Seven & i's ownership below 40% and changed Seven Bank from a consolidated subsidiary to an equity-method affiliate. ITOCHU later acquired a material position and made Seven Bank an equity-method affiliate of ITOCHU as well.

Sources for the following table: [Seven Bank's account of deconsolidation](https://www.sevenbank.co.jp/english/ir/management/president.html), [ITOCHU's 2025 affiliate announcement](https://www.itochu.co.jp/en/news/press/2025/251226.html), and [Seven Bank's shareholder list as of 2026-03-31](https://www.sevenbank.co.jp/english/ir/stock/stock_info.html).

| Date or snapshot | Event / holder | Publicly recorded status |
|---|---|---|
| 2025-06-24 | Seven Bank treasury-share acquisition | Seven Bank ceased to be a Seven & i consolidated subsidiary and became an equity-method affiliate |
| 2025-12 | ITOCHU investment | ITOCHU announced Seven Bank had become its equity-method affiliate after regulatory approvals and share acquisitions |
| 2026-03-31 | Seven-Eleven Japan | 33.38% of Seven Bank shares, excluding treasury stock in the published calculation |
| 2026-03-31 | ITOCHU Corporation | 20.41% of Seven Bank shares, excluding treasury stock in the published calculation |

The percentages above are a dated shareholder snapshot. They should not be reused as perpetual ownership figures without checking Seven Bank's current stock-information page.

## Current operating perimeter

Seven Bank's 2026 company brochure lists Seven Card Service and Seven Payment Service as wholly owned subsidiaries. Seven Bank remains a listed bank and continues to collaborate with Seven & i, but the legal group boundary changed.

Sources for the following table: [Seven Bank's corporate brochure](https://www.sevenbank.co.jp/english/corp/company/outline.html), [Seven Bank's operating overview](https://www.sevenbank.co.jp/english/corp/company/business.html), and [Seven Card Service's company outline](https://www.7card.co.jp/company/com_info/summary.html).

| Entity or layer | Current documented position | Main role |
|---|---|---|
| Seven Bank, Ltd. | TSE-listed bank; equity-method affiliate of Seven & i and ITOCHU based on the cited 2025–2026 records | Domestic ATM platform, retail banking, corporate services, and overseas ATM operations |
| Seven Card Service Co., Ltd. | 100% subsidiary of Seven Bank | Credit-card and electronic-money business, including Seven Card and nanaco |
| Seven Payment Service, Ltd. | 100% subsidiary of Seven Bank | Remittance and settlement services, including ATM-based receipt and collection services |
| Seven CS Card Service Co., Ltd. | Listed by Seven & i in its current corporate and financial structure | Separate legacy card entity; do not merge it with Seven Card Service |
| Seven-Eleven Japan retail channel | Largest published Seven Bank shareholder as of 2026-03-31 and major ATM location partner | Retail touchpoint; not the banking entity |

## Seven Bank platform

Seven Bank describes its domestic ATM platform as its core business. The company also offers account services and corporate functions that use ATMs as a bridge between physical and digital transactions.

Sources for the following table: [Seven Bank's operating overview](https://www.sevenbank.co.jp/english/corp/company/business.html) and [FY2026 principal data](https://www.sevenbank.co.jp/english/ir/finance/service_data/back_num.html).

| Capability | Public description | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic ATM network | About 28,000 ATMs at Seven & i stores and other retail, transit, tourist, and public locations | Installed count is date-sensitive |
| Partner-card transactions | ATMs serve many financial institutions and card networks under separate terms | Do not state a partner count without a dated operating table |
| Cash top-up | Fourth-generation ATMs support top-ups for a growing range of cashless services | Availability varies by payment service |
| Procedures and authentication | +Connect and related ATM functions support specified procedures and identity workflows | Each supported procedure requires current service confirmation |
| Corporate cash receipt and collection | Seven Payment Service provides ATM-based settlement functions | Product terms determine eligible users and transaction flows |

The platform is broader than cash withdrawal, but “ATM infrastructure” should not be equated with universal banking-as-a-service or a prediction that cash use will disappear.

## Card and nanaco layer

Seven Card Service states that it has conducted credit-card business since 2002 and issued and operated nanaco since 2007. It became a Seven Bank subsidiary in 2023.

Sources for the following table: [Seven Card Service's corporate information](https://www.7card.co.jp/company/com_info/), [business directory](https://www.7card.co.jp/company/bs/), and [company outline](https://www.7card.co.jp/company/com_info/summary.html).

| Product / activity | Operator | Current reading |
|---|---|---|
| Seven Card / Seven Card Plus | Seven Card Service | Credit-card products governed by current card terms and enrollment status |
| nanaco | Seven Card Service | Electronic-money business; distinct from bank deposits and from 7iD membership |
| Seven Bank account linkage | Seven Bank and Seven Card Service | Group integration exists, but each product keeps its own agreement and regulatory boundary |
| Retail benefits | Participating Seven & i and other merchants | Rates, eligibility, and participating stores must be checked by date |

## Seven Payment Service

Seven Bank lists Seven Payment Service as a wholly owned subsidiary whose business is remittance and settlement. Its history records the launch of ATM cash-receipt service and later ATM cash-collection service.

Sources for the following table: [Seven Bank's company brochure](https://www.sevenbank.co.jp/english/corp/company/outline.html), [Seven Bank history](https://www.sevenbank.co.jp/english/corp/company/history.html), and [Seven Bank's operating overview](https://www.sevenbank.co.jp/english/corp/company/business.html).

| Service type | Publicly described function | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| ATM cash receipt | Recipient obtains eligible corporate payments through the ATM flow | Not every refund, benefit, or payout program participates |
| ATM cash collection | Eligible corporate cash is deposited through supported ATM workflows | Service contract defines users, limits, and settlement |
| Remittance and settlement | Corporate-payment infrastructure within the Seven Bank group | It is not evidence of general EC acquiring for all merchants |

## YORK Holdings: completed separation

Seven & i formed YORK Holdings for superstore and specialty-store businesses and later completed a transaction with a Bain Capital-related structure. The separation is no longer merely a 2024 plan.

Sources for the following table: Seven & i's [FY2025 consolidated results](https://www.7andi.com/en/ir/file/library/kt/pdf/2026_0409kte.pdf), its [interim transaction accounting disclosure](https://www.7andi.com/en/ir/file/library/kt/pdf/2025_1009kte.pdf), and [2026 corporate-governance description](https://www.7andi.com/en/ir/management/governance/board.html).

| Date | Event | Accounting / perimeter effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2024-10 | Original YORK Holdings established | Intermediate holding-company step |
| 2025-03-06 | Definitive transaction announced with a Bain Capital-related entity | Agreement stage |
| 2025-09-01 | Business divestiture and reinvestment completed | YORK subsidiaries left Seven & i consolidation; the relevant investment became equity-method accounted |

No automatic claim should be made that nanaco stopped working at a separated banner, that card liabilities moved, or that ATM economics changed. Those require product and contract evidence after the transaction.

## Current structural reading

Sources for the following comparison table: [Seven Bank's operating overview](https://www.sevenbank.co.jp/english/corp/company/business.html), [AEON Financial Service domestic operations](https://www.aeonfinancial.co.jp/en/corp/enterprise/), [FamilyMart's 2026 ATM launch](https://www.family.co.jp/company/news_releases/2026/20260601_01.html), and [KDDI's Lawson partnership release](https://newsroom.kddi.com/english/news/detail/kddi_pr_secret-79.html).

| Group / channel | Documented finance center | Key boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Seven & i / Seven Bank | ATM platform, bank accounts, card, nanaco, and settlement subsidiaries connected by an equity-method relationship | Seven Bank is no longer consolidated by Seven & i |
| AEON | AEON Financial Service and AEON Bank distributed through retail and mall locations | Banking and insurance-agency roles are separate |
| FamilyMart | FamiPay and Famima Card plus Seven Bank-operated Famima ATMs | The ATM partner is also an ITOCHU equity-method affiliate, but FamilyMart does not own a bank through that arrangement |
| Lawson | Lawson Bank and retail services plus announced KDDI collaboration | KDDI products are not automatically Lawson-issued |
