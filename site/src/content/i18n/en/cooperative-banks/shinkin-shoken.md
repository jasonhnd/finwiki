# Shinkin Asset Management

> **Page scope note**: The historical slug `shinkin-shoken` is retained for link stability. The verified legal entity covered here is **Shinkin Asset Management Investment Trust Co., Ltd.**; the page does not assert that a separate company named "Shinkin Securities" exists.

## TL;DR

**Shinkin Asset Management Co., Ltd.** is an asset-management company wholly owned by Shinkin Central Bank. Its official profile states that it was established as Zenshinren Investment Advisors in December 1990, adopted its current name in November 1998, and conducts investment management, investment advisory and agency, and Type II financial instruments business. This page therefore does not treat it as a presumed company named “Shinkin Securities” or as an entity that handles registration for individual shinkin banks. ^[Sources: https://www.skam.co.jp/company/information/.]

## 2. Business content

There are three officially recognized business boundaries:

1. **Investment management business** — Management of investment trusts, etc.
2. **Investment advisory/agency** — Advice/representation based on contract.
3. **Type II financial instruments business** — Type II activities within the registered scope.

Check individual funds, sales companies, and assets under management with reference dates in official product materials, sales company lists, and company profiles. It is not assumed that the company will be responsible for the registration and sales of individual credit unions.
