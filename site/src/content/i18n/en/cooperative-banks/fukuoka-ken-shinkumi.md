## TL;DR

Fukuoka Prefectural Credit Union is a prefecture-based regional credit union with its main office located in Akasaka, Chuo-ku, Fukuoka City, Fukuoka Prefecture. FSA registry No.128 (Fukuoka Finance Branch Administration), Corporate Number4290005002505. Fukuoka prefecture3 As the core of the credit unions (Fukuoka Prefecture Doctors Credit Union, Fukuoka Prefecture Credit Union, Fukuoka Prefectural Office Credit Union), we are responsible for SME financing, housing loans, and regional finance within the prefecture.[[cooperative-banks/zenshin-kumi|全国信用協同組合連合会 (全信組連)]]member,[[financial-regulators/dic|預金保険機構]]subject. ### Membership qualification^[extracted]

As it is a prefecture-based regional credit union, the membership qualification is a two-tier structure: **district (prefecturewide) + business area**:

1. **Area requirements**: Individuals and corporations who live, work, or have a business office in Fukuoka Prefecture.
2. **Size requirements**: Small and medium-sized businesses — 300 employees or less or capital 3 億円 or less (depending on industry)
3. **Business area**: No industry specified (cross-industry as it is regional)
