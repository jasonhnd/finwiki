There is no current entity named **“Nagoya Shinkin Bank”** in the Financial Services Agency's shinkin bank license list (as of 2025-11-06). The prior version was withdrawn because it combined a nonexistent entity name, the unrelated `meishin.co.jp` domain, and an unverified head office, business area, and competitive position. To identify a current shinkin bank headquartered in Nagoya, check legal names such as **Aichi Shinkin Bank** and **Chunichi Shinkin Bank** in the FSA list. “The Bank of Nagoya” is a separate ordinary bank, not a shinkin bank. ^[Sources: https://www.fsa.go.jp/menkyo/menkyoj/shinkin.xlsx; https://www.aichishinkin.co.jp/; https://www.shinkin.co.jp/chunichi/about/profile.html.]

## Verification rule

1. Check the official name, corporate number, and head office location in the Financial Services Agency directory.
2. Go to the official domain with the same official name and check the overview, history, and business area.
3. Do not equate ordinary banks, shinkin banks, and credit cooperatives merely because they share a place name.
4. Names that are not in the current directory will not be created on the corporate page unless primary materials such as merger history are available.
