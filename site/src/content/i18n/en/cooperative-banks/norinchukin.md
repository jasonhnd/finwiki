# 農林中央金庫 (Norinchukin Bank)

## Wiki route

This entry sits under [[cooperative-banks/INDEX|cooperative-banks INDEX]]. Read it against [[megabanks/mufg|三菱 UFJ FG (MUFG)]] for peer / contrast context and [[banking/INDEX|banking index]] for the broader system / regulatory boundary.

## TL;DR

The Norinchukin Bank is a **national cooperative financial institution** whose members include JA, JF, JForest, and their federations. It is a private financial institution governed by the Norinchukin Bank Act (平成13年法律第93号). It was established as the Central Bank for Industrial Cooperatives on 1923-12-20 and adopted its current name in 1943. Its official business areas are Food and Agriculture, Retail, Investment, and the Corporate functions that support them. As of 2025-09-30, consolidated total assets were 83兆2,928億円 and membership stood at 3,167 organizations. ^[Sources: https://www.nochubank.or.jp/about/profile.html; https://www.nochubank.or.jp/about/business/.]

### Cooperative-system finance and investment business

- Use the official FAQ and disclosures to verify how surplus funds from savings accepted by JA, JF, and related organizations flow to prefectural federations such as Shinnoren and Shingyoren and then to the Norinchukin Bank.
- The Bank identifies Investment as one of its three core business areas and describes internationally diversified investment and returns to members.
- Because it is neither a statutory central bank nor a lender of last resort, this page does not use central-bank terminology even metaphorically.

### International diversified-investment strategy

- Foreign-currency management centered on **US Treasuries, corporate bonds, and MBS**
- Investment and funding operations at 3 locations: New York, London, and Singapore
- Asset composition, profit and loss, foreign exchange and interest rate risks are confirmed in the official financial statements and disclosures for each period.

### 2024-2025  portfolio reconstruction ^[extracted]

- Policy announced in 2024: realize losses by selling US Treasuries and other holdings (including a plan to sell roughly 10 兆円 of low-yield assets), raise capital (including 7,360 億円 of deferred-dividend capital contributions), and reallocate risk assets
- Reduced distributions to contributing JA organizations and others → an internal governance issue

### Affiliated-finance reorganization

- A central institution in the nationwide JA Bank system (2002〜)
- Reorganization of the role division with prefectural credit federations ([[cooperative-banks/ja-shinnoren|信用農業協同組合連合会]])
- Also serves as a central institution for investment and funding in the fisheries-cooperative system (JF Marine Bank) and forestry-cooperative system (JForest)

### Competitive position

- **Compared with megabanks ([[megabanks/mufg]] / [[megabanks/smfg]] / [[megabanks/mizuho-fg]])**: commercial-banking business is limited; the core business is affiliated finance + institutional investment
- **Compared with life insurers ([[life-insurers/nippon-life]] / [[life-insurers/dai-ichi-life]])**: both act as institutional investors, but any scale comparison must align the reference date and asset definitions in official disclosures
- **Compared with policy financial institutions ([[financial-regulators/jfc]] / [[financial-regulators/dbj]])**: partially competing / complementary in lending to agriculture/forestry/fisheries

## 4. Regulation / policy

- **Competent authorities**: the Financial Services Agency (FSA) + the **Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries** (the affiliated-finance portion) — dual supervision is a feature ^[extracted]
- **Governing law**: the Norinchukin Bank Act (平成13年法律第93号)
- **Recent policy issues**:
  - The appropriateness of the level of dividends / returns to contributing JA
  - Affiliated-finance reorganization (JA Bank consolidation / role division with credit federations)
  - ESG / climate-risk disclosure as an institutional investor

### Representative position

Officer names change over time, so consult the official officer list. As of 2026-07-02, the representative director and president is Taro Kitabayashi; this page does not retain an incumbent named only in an older report.
