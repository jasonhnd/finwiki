# Sapporo Shinkin Bank (integrated into Hokkaido Shinkin Bank in 2018)

## Wiki route

This page preserves the historical Sapporo Shinkin route under [[cooperative-banks/INDEX|cooperative-banks INDEX]]. For the operating institution after 2018-01-01, use **Hokkaido Shinkin Bank** and verify it against [[banking/shinkin-bank-registry-japan|Japan shinkin bank registry]]. Compare [[cooperative-banks/sapporo-chuo-shinkumi|Sapporo Chuo Credit Cooperative]] as a separate cooperative institution; for ordinary-bank comparisons in Hokkaido, see [[regional-banks/hokkaido-bank|Hokkaido Bank]] and [[regional-banks/hokuyo-bank|Hokuyo Bank]].

## TL;DR

Sapporo Shinkin Bank is not its current trade name. Sapporo Shinkin Bank, Hokkai Shinkin Bank, and Otaru Shinkin Bank merged on January 1, 2018, and became **Hokkaido Shinkin Bank**. The Financial Services Agency's 2025-11-06 directory also includes Hokkaido Shinkin Bank (corporate number 3430005003118, head office 3-15-1 Minami 2-jo Nishi, Chuo-ku, Sapporo). Corrected the fact that the previous version described Sapporo Shinkin Bank as an active corporation and listed an unresolvable old domain. ^[Sources: https://www.shinkin.co.jp/hokkaido/bankatm/index.php; https://www.shinkin.co.jp/hokkaido/topinclude/pdf/news/291218gappeininka.pdf; https://www.fsa.go.jp/menkyo/menkyoj/shinkin.xlsx.]

## Interpretation rule

- Do not connect Sapporo Shinkin Bank data before 2017 and Hokkaido Shinkin Bank data after 2018 as the same time series without annotation.
- Although post-merger branches carry the lineages of the 3 former shinkin banks, the current contracting and registered entity is Hokkaido Shinkin Bank.
- Do not publish Sapporo market rankings or claims such as “largest in Hokkaido” without a comparison of all shinkin banks on the same reference date.
