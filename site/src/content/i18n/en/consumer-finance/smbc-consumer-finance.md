# SMBC Consumer Finance

## TL;DR

SMBC Consumer Finance is the consumer-finance operator of the [[megabanks/smfg|SMFG]] group, known for its **PROMISE brand**. Its core businesses are money lending and guarantees, centered on unsecured personal loans and credit-scoring know-how. Its corporate history traces back to Kansai Finance, established in 1962; in 2012 it made PROMISE a wholly owned subsidiary and then adopted its current trade name. Read it separately by role from the card company [[card-issuers/smbc-card|Sumitomo Mitsui Card]].

Note the two-tier parent-subsidiary relationship: the direct shareholder is Sumitomo Mitsui Card, with SMFG standing above it. The INDEX's "SMFG 100% wholly-owned subsidiary" is an expression based on the ultimate parent; the direct holding entity is Sumitomo Mitsui Card.

## 2. Business role

- Within SMFG's retail-finance stack, it handles unsecured consumer loans and guarantees.
- It complements bank accounts, cards, and payment services with direct lending and credit-scoring know-how.
- Card loans under the PROMISE brand are its mainstay.
- Lined up against peers [[consumer-finance/acom|ACOM]] (the [[megabanks/mufg|MUFG]] line) and [[consumer-finance/aiful|AIFUL]] (independent), it establishes a comparison axis for megabank-affiliated consumer lenders.
