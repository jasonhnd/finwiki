## TL;DR

Principal Financial Group, Inc. is a US-headquartered global financial services group focused on retirement, insurance, and asset management, headquartered in Des Moines, Iowa and listed on NASDAQ (PFG). In Japan, the current FSA Financial Instruments Business Operator registry identifies **プリンシパル・グローバル・インベスターズ株式会社** as Kanto Local Finance Bureau (Kinsho) No. 462, registered for Type II financial instruments business, investment advisory / agency business, and investment management business. This page therefore anchors the group's verified Japan asset-management entity rather than inferring a retail-insurance license or an unspecified “Principal Japan” company.

## 2. Operating model

Principal Financial Group's global business spans:

- Retirement and Income Solutions (US-centric)
- Principal Global Investors (asset management, global)
- US Insurance Solutions (life and specialty benefits)
- Principal International (selected non-US markets, historically Latin America and Asia)

In Japan, the registry-confirmed operating boundary is investment management, investment advisory / agency, and Type II financial instruments business. Principal's global profile describes the wider group as spanning retirement, asset management, and insurance; those global lines should not be treated as proof that every product or license is offered through the Japanese registered entity.
