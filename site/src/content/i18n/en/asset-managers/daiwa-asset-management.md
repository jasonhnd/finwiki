## TL;DR

Daiwa Asset Management Co., Ltd. is an asset management company holding **investment management business** registration under the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act, and serves as the asset-management arm of the [[securities-firms/daiwa-sg|Daiwa Securities Group]]. On the FSA Financial Instruments Business Operator Registry, Daiwa Asset Management Co., Ltd. is listed under the Kanto Local Finance Bureau Director (Financial Instruments Business Registration) 第352号. It handles publicly offered investment trusts, discretionary investment management, and ETF / index products, and serves as the fee-based asset management function of the Daiwa Group.

## 2. Operating model

As an investment management business operator, it handles (1) **investment trust delegation** of publicly offered investment trusts (structuring and management), (2) **discretionary investment management** for institutional investors and pension funds, (3) investment advisory services, and (4) ETF / index products. Sales are primarily channeled through Daiwa Securities' broker sales network, connecting to capital relationships with Japan Post Life Insurance and peripheral asset management operations including Global X Japan. This represents the **asset-management fee pillar** separated from the securities group's brokerage / IB functions. Supervision operates under a two-tier structure of the FSA and self-regulatory organizations. It directly competes with [[asset-managers/nomura-asset-management|Nomura Asset]], a fellow securities-affiliated peer.
