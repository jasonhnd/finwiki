## TL;DR

Asset Management One Co., Ltd. is an asset-management operating company holding **Investment Management Business** registration under the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act. In the FSA Financial Instruments Business Operators Registry, it is listed as "Asset Management One Co., Ltd." under the Kanto Finance Bureau Director (Financial Instruments) 第324号. It is a **JV (joint venture)** funded by [[megabanks/mizuho-fg|Mizuho FG]] and [[life-insurers/dai-ichi-life|Dai-ichi Life Group]], which distinguishes it from other affiliate asset-management companies in that it is not simply a wholly-owned subsidiary of a megabank. Its business centers on public mutual funds (public investment trusts) + discretionary investment mandates.

## 2. Operating model

As an investment management business operator, it assumes responsibility for: (1) **Investment Trust Management** for public mutual funds (formation and management of investment trusts), (2) **Discretionary Investment Mandates** for pension and institutional investors (discretionary mandate), and (3) investment advisory services. Distribution spans the banking and trust channels of [[megabanks/mizuho-bank|Mizuho Bank]] and [[trust-banks/mizuho-trust-bank|Mizuho Trust Bank]], as well as the insurance channel of Dai-ichi Life, constituting a **bank-trust-insurance joint platform**. Supervision follows a two-tier structure of FSA + self-regulatory organizations (Investment Trusts Association / Japan Investment Advisers Association). It is distinguished in its equity structure from [[asset-managers/mufg-asset-management|Mitsubishi UFJ AM]], which is a wholly-owned subsidiary of a megabank.
