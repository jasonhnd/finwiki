## TL;DR

Mebuki Lease Co., Ltd. is a regional-bank-affiliated leasing company of [[regional-banks/mebuki-fg|Mebuki FG]] (TSE PRIME 7167) and a 100% subsidiary. It **originated as Joyo Lease, established by [[regional-banks/joyo-bank|Joyo Bank]] in 1974-09, and became Mebuki Lease when Mebuki FG acquired all of its shares and changed its name on 2017-04-03** (confirmed by the official corporate history and corporate registration). On the same date, the leasing business operated by Ashikaga Credit Guarantee, a subsidiary of [[regional-banks/ashikaga-bank|Ashikaga Bank]], was consolidated into Mebuki Lease through business succession; Mebuki Lease then opened Utsunomiya and Ashikaga sales departments and expanded into Tochigi (confirmed by Nikkei dated 2017-03-07 and the official corporate history). **Legally, this was a sole name change of Joyo Lease, not an "equal merger of Joyo Lease + Ashikaga Lease": no independent company named "Ashikaga Lease" existed, and Ashikaga Credit Guarantee operated the Ashikaga-side leasing business.** The company provides equipment leasing, finance leasing, operating leasing, vendor leasing, and related services to mid-sized main-bank customers in Ibaraki, Tochigi, and the Tokyo metropolitan area. It is among the leading regional-bank-affiliated leasing firms. ^[extracted]

### Former Joyo Lease lineage (originating entity)

- **1974-09**: [[regional-banks/joyo-bank|Joyo Bank]] established Joyo Lease Co., Ltd. as a complementary business for its leasing and finance functions (confirmed by the official corporate history)
- As a [[regional-banks/joyo-bank|Joyo Bank]]-affiliated leasing company, its main business was equipment leasing for mid-sized companies in Ibaraki Prefecture
- A typical postwar regional-bank-affiliated lessor: it supported SME capital investment through equipment leasing rather than bank lending
- **2017-04-03**: Mebuki FG acquired all shares and renamed the company Mebuki Lease Co., Ltd. (confirmed by corporate registration)

### Relationship with Ashikaga Bank's leasing function — confirmed (verified 2026-05-30)

- **No independent company named "Ashikaga Lease" existed.** Historically, the [[regional-banks/ashikaga-bank|Ashikaga Bank]]-affiliated leasing company was Kita-Kanto Lease Co., Ltd. (FSA certification of a business-restructuring plan dated 2003-03-11; capital JPY 145 million). In later years, **Ashikaga Credit Guarantee Co., Ltd. operated the leasing business using seconded bank employees** (Nikkei dated 2017-03-07). The former version's framing of an "equal merger of Joyo Lease + Ashikaga Lease" was wrong both about the company name and the form of integration.
- **The succession of the Ashikaga-side leasing business did take place.** On 2017-04-03, **the leasing business of Ashikaga Credit Guarantee was consolidated into Joyo Lease, which was renamed Mebuki Lease on the same date, through business succession** (confirmed by Nikkei dated 2017-03-07 and Mebuki Lease's official corporate history). Mebuki Lease consequently opened Utsunomiya and Ashikaga sales departments and expanded into Tochigi, while Ashikaga Bank continued to second employees. The statement that Mebuki Lease "succeeded to the leasing business of Ashikaga Credit Guarantee" is therefore **confirmed**; however, the successor and form were a business consolidation into Joyo Lease = Mebuki Lease, not an "equal merger with Ashikaga Lease."
- Mebuki FG subsequently acquired all shares of Ashikaga Credit Guarantee on 2020-10-01 and renamed it Mebuki Credit Guarantee Co., Ltd.; it now specializes in credit guarantees (the leasing business had already been transferred to Mebuki Lease in 2017-04) (Nikkei 2020-07 and group-company information).
- [[regional-banks/ashikaga-bank|Ashikaga Bank]] went through temporary nationalization in 2003-11 and was integrated into Mebuki FG in 2016-10; the 2017-04 leasing consolidation described above formed part of the post-integration reorganization of group functions.

### Establishment of Mebuki FG and group integration

- **2016-10-01**: [[regional-banks/mebuki-fg|Mebuki FG]] established through a joint share transfer by [[regional-banks/joyo-bank]] + [[regional-banks/ashikaga-bank]]
- **2017-04-03**: Mebuki FG acquired all shares of Joyo Lease and renamed it Mebuki Lease — reorganizing it as the group's common leasing function for the customer bases of its 2 bank subsidiaries

### Position within the FG

- **Core of bank-leasing coordination**: serves the leasing needs of mid-sized main-bank customers of [[regional-banks/mebuki-fg]]'s 2 banks
- **Capital-investment support**: complements bank lending + addresses off-balance-sheet treatment needs
- **Coordination with local governments and the public sector**: leasing transactions for municipalities in Ibaraki and Tochigi prefectures

### Competitive landscape

- **Major lessors**: [[leasing-firms/mitsubishi-hc-capital|Mitsubishi HC Capital]] / [[leasing-firms/smfl|SMFL]] / [[leasing-firms/mizuho-lease|Mizuho Leasing]] / [[leasing-firms/fuyo-lease|Fuyo General Lease]] / [[leasing-firms/tokyo-century|Tokyo Century]] / [[leasing-firms/ricoh-leasing|Ricoh Leasing]] — advantaged in large-scale, nationwide, and complex transactions
- **Regional-bank-affiliated lessors**: [[leasing-firms/hokkaido-lease|Hokkaido Leasing]] / Chiba Kogyo Lease / Gunma Lease and others — the same type of model
- **Manufacturer-affiliated lessors**: [[card-issuers/toyota-finance|Toyota Finance]] / [[leasing-firms/nec-leasing|NEC Capital Solutions]] — product-linked
- **In-prefecture competition**: leasing affiliated with [[regional-banks/tsukuba-bank|Tsukuba Bank]] and [[regional-banks/tochigi-bank|Tochigi Bank]]

### Strengths and weaknesses

- Strength: the [[regional-banks/joyo-bank]] / [[regional-banks/ashikaga-bank]] customer base (mid-sized main-bank customers in Ibaraki and Tochigi) + locally rooted, on-the-ground service
