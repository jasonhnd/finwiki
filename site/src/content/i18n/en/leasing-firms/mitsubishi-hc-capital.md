## TL;DR

Mitsubishi HC Capital is a major leasing and asset-finance company formed through the **April (month 4) 2021 merger of Mitsubishi UFJ Lease and Hitachi Capital** (securities code 8593). It is connected to the Mitsubishi, MUFG, and Mitsubishi Corporation lineages and, by total assets immediately after the merger, was in the industry's No. 2 tier after [[leasing-firms/orix-corp|ORIX]]. Its activities center on leasing, installment sales, and money lending (asset finance), extending to international assets including aircraft, logistics, energy, and real estate.

The merger combined Mitsubishi UFJ Lease's strengths in Japan, Mitsubishi Corporation/MUFG links, and assets such as aircraft engines with Hitachi Capital's strengths in overseas business, Hitachi Group links, and vendor finance.

## 2. Operating model

As a lessor, it manages equipment, aviation, logistics, and energy assets over long periods where a bank balance sheet alone may be less suitable. Its businesses broadly include:

- **Customer Solutions**: leasing and finance for Japanese companies and public bodies.
- **Vendor finance / sales finance**: sales support for manufacturers and distributors (a Hitachi-related strength).
- **Aviation / Logistics**: leasing of international assets such as aircraft, engines, containers, and rail freight cars.
- **Environment & Energy / Real Estate / Mobility**: asset finance related to energy, real estate, and mobility.

## 3. Strategic read

Mitsubishi HC Capital is the adjacent asset-finance function of the [[megabanks/mufg|MUFG]] and Mitsubishi Corporation networks and can be compared with [[leasing-firms/orix-corp|ORIX]], [[leasing-firms/tokyo-century|東京センチュリー]], [[leasing-firms/fuyo-lease|芙蓉総合リース]], and [[leasing-firms/smfl|SMFL]]. Whereas ORIX has the character of a broad independent conglomerate spanning insurance, banking, real estate, and U.S. private equity, Mitsubishi HC Capital has stronger affinity with large companies, global assets, and Mitsubishi/MUFG networks. The incorporation of the former Hitachi Capital overseas platform means the merger was aimed at geographic and business complementarity as well as scale.
