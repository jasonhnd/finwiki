## TL;DR

**IBJ Leasing (興銀リース)** was established in 1969 as a leasing company affiliated with the former Industrial Bank of Japan (IBJ). For many years, it operated as a leading leasing company in the Mizuho / former IBJ lineage. **On 2019-04-01, the company changed its name to "Mizuho Leasing" → becoming [[leasing-firms/mizuho-lease|Mizuho Leasing / みずほリース]], and "IBJ Leasing" ceased to exist as a separate corporate name.** This page is therefore a **historical-entity + naming-transition note**. For current business / financial information, refer to [[leasing-firms/mizuho-lease|Mizuho Leasing]].

## 2. Name-change history & business lines (former)

The former IBJ Leasing coexisted within the Mizuho lineage as an upper-mid-tier leasing company with strengths in **real estate, aircraft, and large-scale project finance**, while partly overlapping in business scope with the Fuyo Group's [[leasing-firms/fuyo-lease|Fuyo General Lease]]. It inherited the former IBJ's **large-corporate / project-finance** DNA and demonstrated particular strength in large transactions involving real estate, ships, aircraft, and overseas projects.

Principal former business areas:

- General equipment and industrial equipment leasing
- Real estate leasing and finance (large properties)
- Operating leases and JOL for aircraft and ships
- Overseas operations (mainly in Asia)
- Environmental and renewable-energy project finance

### Background to the 2019-04 name change to Mizuho Leasing

- Leasing functions within Mizuho FG overlapped across IBJ Leasing + Fuyo General Lease and other companies, creating pressure for intra-group reorganization
- The IBJ Leasing → Mizuho Leasing name change (2019-04-01) formed part of Mizuho FG's reorganization of its non-bank functions
- In 2024, Marubeni's participation as a strategic shareholder in [[leasing-firms/mizuho-lease|Mizuho Leasing]] was announced, advancing its evolution into a Mizuho-affiliated + trading-house-affiliated hybrid platform

## 3. Strategic significance of this transition

The former IBJ Leasing's transition reflected Mizuho FG's conclusion that there was little benefit in retaining multiple leasing subsidiaries. Meanwhile, the Fuyo Group's [[leasing-firms/fuyo-lease|Fuyo General Lease]] continues to operate in parallel as an independent listed company, so leasing consolidation within the broader Mizuho lineage remains incomplete.

This history is an important case for understanding [[megabanks/mizuho-fg|Mizuho FG]]'s non-bank strategy: the process of combining the 3 legacies of the former IBJ, Fuji Bank, and Dai-Ichi Kangyo Bank.

In the context of [[finance/japan-acquisition-finance|acquisition finance in Japan]], the former IBJ Leasing's large-project expertise was carried forward by Mizuho Leasing and continues to be used in Mizuho-affiliated LBO / M&A finance.
