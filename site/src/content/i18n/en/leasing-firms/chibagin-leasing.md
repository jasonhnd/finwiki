## TL;DR

Chibagin Leasing Co., Ltd. is a general leasing company in the [[regional-banks/chiba-bank|Chiba Bank (TSE PRIME 8331)]] group and operates primarily in Chiba Prefecture. Chiba Bank's public material dated 2022-08-05 identifies it as a "group company." This page does not describe it as a "100% subsidiary of Chiba Bank on a standalone basis" without current material showing the breakdown of direct and indirect voting rights.
