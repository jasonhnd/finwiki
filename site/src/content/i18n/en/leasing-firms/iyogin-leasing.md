## TL;DR

Iyogin Leasing Co., Ltd. is a regional-bank-affiliated leasing company in the [[regional-banks/iyogin-hd|Iyogin HD (TSE PRIME 5830)]] group. Backed by the mid-sized-company main-bank customer base of core subsidiary [[regional-banks/iyo-bank|Iyo Bank]] (established in 1878 as the Twenty-Ninth National Bank; No.1 share in Ehime Prefecture), it provides equipment leasing, finance leasing, operating leasing, municipal leasing, medical leasing, **maritime-cluster (Imabari Shipbuilding + shipping) equipment leasing**, agricultural-machinery leasing, auto leasing, and other services across **Ehime Prefecture + all of Shikoku + mainland locations including Osaka, Tokyo, and Hiroshima**. It is among the largest regional-bank-affiliated leasing firms across Shikoku's 4 prefectures and was repositioned as one of the group's parallel subsidiaries after [[regional-banks/iyogin-hd]] adopted a holding-company structure on 2022-10-03. ^[extracted]
