# Hamagin Finance

## Wiki route

This entry sits under the [[leasing-firms/INDEX|leasing-firms INDEX]] as the leasing and finance operating-company entry for the [[regional-banks/yokohama-bank|Bank of Yokohama]] group. ## TL;DR

The official entity responsible for leasing in the Bank of Yokohama group is **Hamagin Finance Co., Ltd.** It was established as Yokohama Finance on 1979-09-20 and adopted its current name in 1984-05. Its official company information lists leasing and installment sales, financial operations including monetary loan agreements, payment collection, factoring, and non-life insurance agency services ([Hamagin Finance company information](https://h-fc.co.jp/company)).
