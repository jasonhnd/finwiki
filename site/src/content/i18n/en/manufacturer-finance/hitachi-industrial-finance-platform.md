# Hitachi Industrial Finance Platform (日立グループ産業金融プラットフォーム)

## TL;DR

The name "Hitachi Industrial Finance Platform" on this page is not an official corporate name or business-segment name, but a FinWiki analytical route for reading the interface between Hitachi, Ltd.'s products/services and finance. The former [[leasing-firms/hitachi-capital|日立キャピタル]] merged with Mitsubishi UFJ Lease on 2021-04-01 to become [[leasing-firms/mitsubishi-hc-capital|三菱HCキャピタル]], and as of that same date ceased to be an affiliated company of Hitachi. Therefore, the current Hitachi must not be treated as "holding Mitsubishi HC Capital as a captive." Current customer finance must be verified as deal-by-deal, region-by-region collaboration with banks, investors, SPVs, policy finance, and the like. A recent officially confirmable example is the NextGen collaboration in which Hitachi ZeroCarbon and MUFG Bank combine funding for electric vehicles and charging infrastructure.

## 4. How to read

- **Confirm the capital boundary first**: Separate the history of the former Hitachi Capital from Hitachi's current consolidation scope.
- **Verify deal by deal**: For railway, power, data centers, etc., the finance structure differs by contract, country, and time even within the same business line.
- **Separate billing models from finance**: Subscription / deferred revenue does not, by itself, mean customer finance or lending.
- **Do not generalize partnerships**: NextGen is a confirmed individual collaboration, not a standard finance scheme for all Hitachi products.

## 5. Regulation / policy

- As a listed company, subject to disclosure under the TSE, the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act, the Companies Act, and IFRS.
- Regulation of banking, leasing, securitization, and export finance follows the financial entities actually involved and the transaction forms.
- Read business regulation (railway, power, nuclear, export control, etc.) separately from the financial regulation applying to the fund providers.
