# BitTrade Inc. — Japan crypto-asset exchange operator overview

> FSA registration No. 00007, Kanto Local Finance Bureau · JVCEA member #1007 · registration date 2017-09-29

## 1. Corporate / shareholders

- **Trade name**: BitTrade Inc. (Japanese: ビットトレード株式会社)
  - Former trade names: BitTrade Inc. → Huobi Japan Inc. (renamed 2018-12-17) → BitTrade Inc. (renamed 2023-04-21)
- **Established**: September 2016
- **Head office location**: 〒105-0003 1-1-1 Nishi-Shimbashi, Minato-ku, Tokyo
- **Capital**: JPY 100 million (as of the end of February 2023)
- **Listing status**: Unlisted
- **Major shareholders (disclosed)**:
  - SINOHOPE SG PTE. LTD. (presumed top shareholder)
  - Sinohope Technology Holdings Limited
  - Tokai Tokyo Financial Holdings, Inc.
  - FPG Co., Ltd.

## 2. License / registration status

- **Crypto-asset exchange business**: Director-General of the Kanto Local Finance Bureau, registration No. 00007
- **Type I Financial Instruments Business**: Director-General of the Kanto Local Finance Bureau, Financial Instruments registration No. 3295
- **JVCEA member**: Type I member #1007 (covering both crypto-asset exchange business and crypto-asset derivatives transactions)
- **JCBA member**: Regular member of the Japan Crypto-asset Business Association
- **Fintech Association**: Venture member
- **Japan STO Association / Japan Security Token Association**: Supporting member

## 3. Listed currencies

- **Listed assets**: the lineup changes through listings and delistings, so the current trading screen and transaction guide take precedence. Historical fixed counts and planned delistings are not carried forward as the current lineup.
- **Features**:
  - Handles multiple altcoins, but historical “most in Japan” or “No. 1” labels are not treated as a current rank
  - Also lists several proprietary / project-based assets (JOC, UPC, CRTS, ZPG, etc.)
  - Operates an IEO (Initial Exchange Offering) platform

## 7. Strategic developments (recent 12  months)

- **July 2025**: Began joint research with JTB and NTT TechnoCross on regional revitalization through "oshi-katsu" (fan activities) (press release)
- **August 2025**: Concluded a basic agreement with Hokubo Co., Ltd. on crypto-asset / Web3.0-related business
- **November 2025**: Agreed with the ENI Foundation to advance collaboration in the Web3.0 domain (press release)
- **January 2026**: Concluded a strategic alliance with Australian real-estate developer Avantaus Development (ADG) leveraging RWA (real-world assets)
- **IEO rollout**: Continues regular information disclosure for domestic proprietary assets (such as JOC); the new-listing application window is also open
- **Use of Type I Financial Instruments Business**: Provides a CFD (crypto-asset derivatives) platform. Discloses its capital-adequacy ratio quarterly (disclosed through the end of March 2026)
