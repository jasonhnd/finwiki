# GMO Coin, Inc. — Japan crypto-asset exchange operator overview

> FSA registration number Kanto Local Finance Bureau 第00006号 · JVCEA member #1006 · registration date 2017-09-29

## 1. Corporation / shareholders

- **Trade name**: GMOコイン株式会社 / GMO Coin, Inc.
- **Date of establishment**: October 2016
- **Head-office location**: 〒150-0043  Shibuya Fukuras (SHIBUYA FUKURAS), 1-2-3  Dogenzaka, Shibuya-ku, Tokyo
- **Capital**: JPY 3.758 billion (including capital reserves; confirmed on the official company page on 2026-07-29)
- **Parent company**: GMO Financial Holdings, Inc. (a 100% subsidiary of that company)
- GMO Financial Holdings is an intermediate holding company positioned under GMO Internet Group, Inc. (TSE Prime 9449). A financial sub-group that brings together the crypto-asset, FX, and foreign-exchange businesses.
- Listing status: unlisted (the parent GMO Internet Group is listed)
- **Corporate number**: 7011001113188

## 2. License / registration status

- **Crypto-asset exchange business**: Director-General of the Kanto Local Finance Bureau 第00006号 (registration date 2017-09-29)
- **Financial instruments business**: Director-General of the Kanto Local Finance Bureau (Kinsho) 第3188号
  - The Kinsho registration for conducting crypto-asset derivative transactions (crypto-asset FX / exchange leverage)
- **JVCEA member**: Type-1 member #1006 (both crypto-asset exchange business + crypto-asset derivative transaction qualifications)
- **Member associations**: Japan Virtual and Crypto assets Exchange Association (JVCEA), Financial Futures Association of Japan (FFAJ)
  - FFAJ membership is because it also conducts the foreign-exchange FX business (financial futures business)

## 3. Handled currencies

- **Crypto assets across services**: the official “What you can do with GMO Coin” page displayed 24 assets when checked on 2026-07-29. Eligibility differs among the broker, exchange, and crypto-asset FX products, so each current product page must be checked.
- **Broker service**: the broker page listed 19 assets on the same date. GMO Coin began handling the Zipangcoin series (ZPG/ZPGAG/ZPGPT), linked to gold, silver, and platinum prices, on 2026-04-20.
- **End of DAI support**: trading on the broker and exchange ended on 2026-06-06, and transfers ended on 2026-06-27. Proceeds from the mandatory sale of residual balances were credited in JPY on 2026-07-02, so DAI is not a current supported asset.
- **Exchange and staking**: eligible assets for spot, leverage, and staking are maintained on product-specific pages. Historical fixed lists should not be treated as the current full lineup.
- The service also handles past IEO assets such as NOT A HOTEL COIN (NAC) and FCR Coin.

## 4. Scope of business

- **Spot trading**: broker service (two-way price) and exchange (order-book trading). Consult the current fee page for maker and taker fees
- **Derivatives**: leveraged exchange trading and crypto-asset FX. Consult current product rules for leverage and margin conditions
- **Foreign-exchange FX**: offered as a financial instruments business. Consult the current product page for currency pairs, leverage, and other conditions
- **Crypto-asset lending**: Basic and Premium products are described. Consult the current product page for terms, rates, and availability
- **Staking**: rewards are distributed based on eligible asset holdings; supported assets and distribution terms are updated on the official page.
- **Crypto-asset accumulation (つみたて)**: accumulation service (from 500円~)
- **IEO**: previously conducted with FCR, NAC, and others
- **Corporate services**: corporate-account support, API trading (Public/Private), WebTrader (high-functionality trading tool)
- **Deposit channels**: bank transfer (GMO Aozora Net Bank recommended), instant deposit (Pay-easy, internet banking, etc.), instant withdrawal (started February 9, 2026)
- **Crypto assets as collateral**: from 2026-07-25, BTC, ETH, XRP, and SOL can be posted as collateral for exchange leverage and crypto-asset FX. At launch the collateral valuation rate was 50% for each asset, with price-risk, shortfall, and forced-sale rules.

## 5. Market share / trading volume

- Published monthly trading volume / number of accounts: not disclosed (no standalone IR disclosure)
- Figures are included as the "crypto-asset business" in GMO Internet Group's consolidated financial results, but GMO Coin's standalone trading volume is not regularly disclosed
- Public materials do not establish GMO Coin's standalone domestic rank. What can be compared is its product breadth—spot, crypto-asset derivatives, foreign-exchange FX, lending, and staking within one company—not a market-share rank. For the regulatory boundary of staking and lending, see [[exchanges/jp-cex-staking-lending-regulation|JP CEX staking and lending regulation]].
- Customer base: mainly individual retail investors. Also accommodates trader / institutional demand through corporate accounts and API provision

## 7. Strategic trends

- **Product-line changes**: in 2026, GMO Coin added SUI, WILD, and the ZPG series and ended support for DAI. Because both additions and removals occur, historical asset counts should not be fixed as current totals.
- **Foreign-exchange FX expansion**: added seven pairs, including HUF/JPY and NOK/SEK, in May 2026.
- **Instant deposit/withdrawal infrastructure**: deposits via linkage with GMO Aozora Net Bank, with instant-withdrawal support from February 2026
- **Using crypto assets as collateral**: the service launched on 2026-07-25 lets customers transfer BTC, ETH, XRP, and SOL into collateral for leveraged trading. Eligible assets, valuation rates, and risk terms remain subject to the official rules.
- **Group DLT / stablecoin activity**: GMO Internet Group conducts a US stablecoin business through the separate entity GMO-Z.com Trust Company, Inc. This is distinct from GMO Coin's Japanese exchange business.
