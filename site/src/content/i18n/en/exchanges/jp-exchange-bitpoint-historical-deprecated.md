> Former FSA registration: Kanto Local Finance Bureau No. 00009 · former JVCEA Type I member · absorbed into SBI VC Trade on 2026-04-01 (surviving company: SBI VC Trade)

**deprecated VASP / hacking victim in 2019-07  (approx. 35 億円 outflow) / corporate entity extinguished by the SBI VC Trade absorption-type merger in 2026-04 **

## 1. Corporate entity / shareholders (final state)

- Trade name: BITPoint Japan Co., Ltd. (English: BITPoint Japan Co., Ltd.)
- Established: March 3, 2016
- Final parent company: SBI Holdings. The SBI Group acquired 51% in 2022 and the remaining 49% in March 2023. Remixpoint was the former parent, not the parent immediately before the merger. ^[Source: https://www.sbigroup.co.jp/news/2023/0214_13601.html.]
- Corporate number: 6011001109930
- Head-office location: 4-2-3 Toranomon, Minato-ku, Tokyo (as stated in the merger notice)

## 2. Licensing / registration status (history)

- Crypto-asset exchange business: **Director-General of the Kanto Local Finance Bureau 第00009号** (registration date 2017-09-29; among the early registrants registered on the same date as bitFlyer)
- Former JVCEA Type I member. This entry does not repeat its former member number, avoiding confusion with BTCBOX's current #1008
- The April 1, 2026 absorption-type merger into SBI VC Trade (No. 00011) dissolved the company and transferred its rights and obligations to the survivor
- The VCTRADE and BITPOINT brands continue for the time being after the merger, with future unification planned

## 3. Handled currencies

- Final lineup: BTC, ETH, BCH, LTC, XRP, TRX, ADA, JMY, BAT, IOST, ATOM, KLAY, MATIC, DEP, LINK, DOT, SHIB, CHZ, FLR, SOL, AVAX, ASTR, XYM, etc. — 20+ tokens
- Characteristics: holds early-listed tokens of the SaaS / metaverse family such as DEP (DEAPCOIN), KLAY (Klaytn), and JMY (Jasmy). Also has a track record of the first domestic listing of FLR (Flare)
- After the merger, the BITPOINT and VCTRADE services continue separately for the time being, with future unification planned

## 4. Scope of business (before the merger)

- Spot sales counter / spot exchange (order-book trading)
- Leveraged trading (offered at one point, later scaled back)
- Crypto-asset lending
- Corporate-oriented services: corporate accounts / OTC support
- Fiat-currency deposits: bank transfer (SBI Sumishin Net Bank and others)

## 5. Market share / trading volume

- This entry has not identified current official figures that compare trading volume or account counts with peers using the same period and definition
- Historical Remixpoint segment disclosures are not treated as BITPOINT's standalone market rank immediately before the merger
- Customer base: centered on individual retail. Corporate-oriented business was limited

## 7. Strategic developments (the path to the merger)

- **2019 unauthorized outflow**: FSA and SESC materials confirm the incident. Compensation and remediation should be evaluated from contemporaneous company disclosures without adding unsupported causal claims.
- **Ownership transfer to the SBI Group**: SBI acquired 51% in 2022 and the remaining 49% in 2023.
- **2026 merger**: the official announcement cites concentration of group resources, operating efficiency, profitability, customer convenience, and new services. BITPOINT and VCTRADE continue as two brands for the time being.
