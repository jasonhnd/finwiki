## Overview

Global CEX ranks, trading volumes, listed assets, and scores change with time and with each data provider's methodology. This page does not freeze a top-ten list at one point in time. Instead, it identifies major public ranking entry points and explains how to read them. When citing a comparison, record the provider, retrieval timestamp, and market scope (spot or derivatives).

## Checks for comparisons

- Separate spot and derivatives rankings.
- Attach a retrieval timestamp to live values such as 24-hour trading volume.
- Treat score and Trust Score as provider-specific metrics.
- For Japanese registration and warning status, consult [[exchanges/fsa-foreign-exchange-warning-system|the FSA warning system]] and the current FSA registries.
