# GOPAX (고팍스) — Korean CEX / Binance investment overview

> Launched 2017-11 · operated by Streami (스트리미) · **Binance announced an investment in GOPAX on 2023-02-03** · GoFi (GOPAX Earn) was affected by Genesis Global's liquidity suspension

## 1. Corporation / Shareholders

- Trade name: 주식회사 스트리미 (Streami Inc.) — holding / operating company
- Established: 2015-04 (Streami founded) / 2017-11 (GOPAX exchange launched)
- Head office location: 서울특별시 강남구 (Gangnam-gu, Seoul)
- Founder: 이준행 (Lee Joon-haeng) — Streami founder, started from a blockchain remittance business
- **Investment-disclosure boundary**:
  - **Binance** announced an investment in GOPAX through the Industry Recovery Initiative on **2023-02-03**
  - Binance's official announcement explains that the investment was intended to support GoFi users but does not state an ownership percentage
  - This entry does not infer current control from reported ownership percentages or unfinished regulatory procedures

## 2. Registration / License status (Korean system)

- **가상자산사업자 (VASP)** registration: registered with FIU in Korea's first 2021 registration cohort
- **ISMS / ISMS-P certification**: holds KISA ISMS-P
- **실명확인 입출금 계정** (real-name account): contract with **전북은행 (Jeonbuk Bank, JB금융그룹 / JB Financial Group)**
  - 전북은행 is a regional bank, and GOPAX's fiat rail depends on this real-name account agreement
  - Bank-side AML/CFT checks and exchange-side VASP obligations are treated separately
- **Binance investment**: the 2023-02-03 official announcement and KoFIU filing status are separate evidence; the investment announcement alone does not establish the outcome of a change notification
- For the detailed regulatory system, see [[exchanges/korea-cex-five-pillars-comparison|韓国 5 大 CEX 制度比較]]

## 3. Listed currencies

- KRW spot market (legal as one of Korea's 5  major CEXs, 1 )
- Listings change over time, so this entry does not fix a count or rank the breadth of peer offerings
- Centered on BTC / ETH / major altcoins
- Listing review: DAXA common standards + GOPAX's own standards

## 4. Scope of business

- Spot trading (centered on KRW pairs)
- Derivatives / margin trading: **not offered under Korean VASP regulation**
- **GoFi (GOPAX Earn)**: a lending product; at the end of 2022  a **Genesis Global bankruptcy-exposure incident** occurred (the GoFi withdrawal-freeze situation arose due to the impact of the bankruptcy of global lending operators such as Genesis Capital)
- staking: limited service
- Fiat deposits: only via 전북은행 real-name account
- B2B / institutional: API trading, OTC

## 7. Strategic developments

- **Binance investment disclosure boundary**: the 2023-02-03 official announcement establishes an investment and support for GoFi users; it does not establish a current ownership percentage or regulatory approval status
- **GoFi response**: the effect of Genesis Global's liquidity suspension on GoFi is examined as a case of external lending-counterparty dependence and user protection
- **전북은행 relationship**: the real-name account agreement is treated as a fiat rail, with bank-side and VASP-side AML/CFT responsibilities examined separately
- **DAXA participation**: standardizing listing criteria at DAXA together with [[exchanges/cex-upbit-korea|Upbit]] / [[exchanges/cex-bithumb-korea|Bithumb]] / [[exchanges/cex-coinone-korea|Coinone]] / [[exchanges/cex-korbit-korea|Korbit]]
- **2024  User Protection Act response**: contributing to the user-protection fund, strengthening unfair-trading surveillance, rebuilding internal controls
- **Relationship with Binance**: future liquidity, listing, or technology integration is not treated as implemented; the scope is limited to confirmed investment disclosure

## 8. Positioning in the industry

GOPAX is a case in which a global CEX invested in a VASP filed in Korea, useful for examining disclosure boundaries among regulators, investors, and the domestic operator.

Within [[exchanges/korea-cex-five-pillars-comparison|Korean CEX system comparison]], KoFIU filing status, real-name accounts, and investment disclosure are treated as separate evidence. This entry does not fix reported ranks, ownership percentages, or a future regulatory outcome.

The GoFi incident is also important in the context of [[exchanges/global-crypto-exchange-bankruptcy-comparison|글로벌 CEX 파산 비교]] as a case of the **pattern in which the insolvency of a global lending operator spreads contagiously to a Korean CEX's subsidiary lending product**.
