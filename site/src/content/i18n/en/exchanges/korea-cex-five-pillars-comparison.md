# Korea's 5  major CEX regimes compared — Upbit / Bithumb / Coinone / Korbit / GOPAX

## Overview of Korea's regime

Under the amended Act on Reporting and Using Specified Financial Transaction Information, Korean virtual-asset service providers file reports with the Korea Financial Intelligence Unit (KoFIU) under the Financial Services Commission (FSC). ISMS certification is one filing condition, and an exchange offering won-denominated trading needs a bank-issued real-name deposit and withdrawal account. A coin-to-coin operator without such an account is not automatically "unregistered." The banking relationships below are a snapshot of operator and bank disclosures reviewed on 2026-07-29.

## Real-name account bank contracts (실명확인 입출금 계정)

A VASP offering won-denominated exchange services must include a bank real-name deposit and withdrawal account in its filing materials. The bank partner is not set by a formal "five major banks" rule; it can change when contracts are made or renewed. Read the table as a dated snapshot.

## ISMS-CASP certification

KISA operates the information-security management-system certification used as a VASP filing condition. ISMS-P is a separate integrated privacy certification; this entry does not claim it is mandatory above a KRW 10 billion trading-volume threshold. Certificate validity and KoFIU report acceptance must be checked separately.

## International comparison

The table compares only high-level responsible bodies and registration frameworks. It does not reduce Korea to a "single regulator" or Japan to simple "dual supervision." ^[Sources: https://www.fsc.go.kr/eng/index; https://www.kofiu.go.kr/eng/main.do; https://www.fsa.go.jp/policy/virtual_currency/index.html; https://www.fincen.gov/msb-state-selector.]

| Item | Korea | Japan | US |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main responsible bodies | FSC / KoFIU, KISA, partner banks | FSA / Local Finance Bureaus, certified self-regulatory bodies | Federal and state authorities |
| Main exchange framework | VASP report to KoFIU (2021-) | [[exchanges/fsa-vasp-registration-system|Crypto-asset exchange operator registration]] (2017-) | FinCEN MSB registration + state requirements, etc. |
| Certification | KISA ISMS | FSA inspection + JVCEA self-regulation | By state |
| Fiat account | Real-name deposit and withdrawal account for won-market operators | Individual bank contracts plus exchange regulation | Federal / state law and bank review |

Korea's real-name-account requirement is an important condition for entering the won market, but this entry does not attribute a five-company market structure to legislation alone. Check KoFIU's latest list for operator counts and filing status.
