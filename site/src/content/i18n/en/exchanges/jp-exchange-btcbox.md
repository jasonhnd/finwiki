# BTCBOX Co., Ltd. — Japan crypto-asset exchange operator overview

> FSA registration number Kanto Local Finance Bureau 第00008号 · JVCEA member #1008 · registration date 2017-09-29

## 1. Entity · shareholders

- **Trade name**: BTCボックス株式会社
- **English name**: BtcBox Co.,Ltd.
- **Founded**: 2014年3月6日 (established by David Zhang with capital of 500万円)
- **Head office**: 〒103-0025  Tokyo, Chuo-ku, Nihonbashi-Kayabacho 2-chome 8-ban 1号, BRICK GATE Kayabacho 5F (relocated in 2018年1月17日 from the former address at Kyobashi 1-chome)
- **Capital**: 22億2704万円 (including capital reserves) — the publicly stated figure after the capital increases from 2019年 onward
- **Parent company**: TTXHoldings Co., Ltd. (in 2019年3月, David Zhang transferred all shares to make it the full parent. TTXHoldings itself is an unlisted, non-public company)
- **Listing status**: unlisted
- **Corporate number**: 7020001104824 (National Tax Agency corporate-number disclosure site. Number assigned 2015年10月5日)

## 2. License · registration status

- **Crypto-asset exchange business**: Director-General of the Kanto Local Finance Bureau 第00008号 (FSA, registration date 2017年9月29日)
- **JVCEA membership**: Type 1 member #1008 (crypto-asset exchange business only. Does not hold the Type-2-equivalent license related to derivatives transaction business)
- **Japan Payment Service Association**: member number 第00745号

## 3. Handled currencies

- Number of handled tokens: 7 tokens (as of 2026-05-18, per the official blog history)
- Main tokens: BTC · ETH · BCH · LTC · DOGE (added 2022年4月) · DOT (added 2022年6月) · TRX (added 2022年7月)
- **Characteristics**: a BTC-centric spot-only exchange. BTC has been handled since the very start of the service in 2014年4月. BCH has the track record of being among the first in Japan to begin trading, in 2017年8月. The pace of altcoin additions is relatively slow, maintaining a small-scale spot-specialized policy.
- Sales counter (Kantan Baibai / easy buy-sell): service began from 2020年10月

## 4. Business scope

- **Spot exchange**: exchange format (order-book trading)
- **Sales counter (Kantan Baibai)**: began 2020年10月
- **Derivatives / margin trading**: none (JVCEA Type 1 · crypto-asset exchange business only)
- **Crypto-asset financing (lending)**: launched in February 2016. Borrowing limits and eligibility conditions can change; consult the current product description and contract terms
- **Anshin Teiki Kounyu (peace-of-mind recurring purchase)**: began 2021年9月 (accumulation service)
- **Fiat deposit**: bank transfer and instant deposit, the latter launched in February 2020. Consult current deposit guidance for supported institutions and fees
- **eKYC**: Speedy Identity Verification began 2020年4月
- **Supported languages**: Japanese · English · Chinese (3 languages)

## 7. Strategic developments

- Within the official materials designated for this review and checked on 2026-07-29, no new-token addition after TRX in July 2022 or major capital measure was identified. This is not an assertion that none exists.
- Maintains a small-scale, independent, spot-only line. No publicly announced plans for feature expansion such as derivatives or staking.
- Has an operating history of more than 10 years since its April 2014 service launch.

## 8. Related people

- **Harutoshi Tsuji**: Representative Director and President (took office 2019年5月)
- **David Zhang (Zhang Dawei)**: founder, former Representative Director. An engineer of Chinese origin. Established the company in 2014年3月. Became Chairman in 2019年5月. Transferred all shares in 2019年3月 to TTXHoldings, which he founded himself.
- **Advising law firm**: Anderson Mori & Tomotsune
- **Accounting auditor**: Zeros Audit Corporation
