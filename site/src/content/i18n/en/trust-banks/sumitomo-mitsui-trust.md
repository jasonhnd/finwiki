# Sumitomo Mitsui Trust Group (SuMi TRUST)

## Wiki route

This entry sits under [[trust-banks/INDEX|trust-banks INDEX]]. Read it against [[trust-banks/master-trust-bank|Nippon Master Trust Bank]] for peer / contrast context and [[banking/INDEX|banking index]] for the broader system / regulatory boundary.

## TL;DR

A trust bank-centered financial holding company independent from megabanks, **3 one of the major trusts** (Mitsubishi UFJ Trust / Sumitomo Mitsui Trust / Mizuho Trust). 2002-02 Chuo Mitsui Trust HD established → 2011-04 merged with Sumitomo Trust Bank → Sumitomo Mitsui Trust Holdings established → **2024-10-01 changed corporate name to Sumitomo Mitsui Trust Group**. Operates pension trusts, real estate trusts, shareholder registry services, testamentary trusts, and more. FY2025  net income attributable to parent company shareholders is **3,175.66 億円**, and total assets as of 2026-03  end are **82.17 兆円**. **Note: [[megabanks/smfg]] (Sumitomo Mitsui FG) is a different legal entity** — Although the "Sumitomo Mitsui" name is shared, SuMi TRUST is the trust lineage of the former Mitsui and Sumitomo, while SMFG is the banking lineage, and they are separate corporate groups. ^[三井住友トラストグループ「2026年3月期決算短信」: https://www.smtg.jp/-/media/tg/investors/fs/2603/2603_01.pdf]

### Merger History and Predecessors

- **Former Mitsui Trust Bank**: 1924  founded (former Mitsui zaibatsu-affiliated trust, one of Japan's earliest trust company lineages)
- **Former Sumitomo Trust Bank**: 1925  founded (former Sumitomo zaibatsu-affiliated trust)
- **Former Chuo Trust Bank**: Established in postwar period, later merged with Mitsui Trust
- **Chuo Mitsui Trust Bank**: 2000  Mitsui Trust Bank + Chuo Trust Bank merger
- **Chuo Mitsui Trust HD**: 2002-02  established (holding company of Chuo Mitsui Trust + Chuo Mitsui Asset Trust)
- **Sumitomo Mitsui Trust HD**: 2011-04  Chuo Mitsui Trust HD + Sumitomo Trust Bank integration

> **Background of integration**: Sumitomo Trust Bank initially pursued an independent path, but postwar merger pressures in the industry and the strategic alignment of maintaining independence as a trust-exclusive entity led to an announcement of management integration policy with Chuo Mitsui Trust HD at 2009 年. Integration occurred through stock exchange at 2011-04 , establishing the largest scale as a trust-exclusive FG. ^[likely]

### Trust-Exclusive Independent Path

- **Independent from megabanks**: Of the 3  major trusts, Mitsubishi UFJ Trust is under [[megabanks/mufg]], Mizuho Trust is under [[megabanks/mizuho-fg]]. **SuMi TRUST alone is an independent holding company from a megabank and is trust-exclusive**. This is its identity and differentiation axis.
- **"Sumitomo Mitsui" name confusion risk**: [[megabanks/smfg]] (Sumitomo Mitsui FG = SMBC line) shares the name, but is a separate legal entity and separate capital. SMFG is a banking lineage from former Taiyou Kobe Bank + former Sumitomo Bank, while SuMi TRUST is a trust lineage from former Mitsui Trust + former Sumitomo Trust.

### Shift Toward Affluent and Corporate Asset Management

- **Individual affluent clients**: Leveraging customer base from former Mitsui and Sumitomo families through testamentary trusts, asset succession, and inheritance planning
- **Corporations**: Large enterprise customer base through pension asset trusteeship (DB/DC), real estate trusts, and shareholder registry services
- **Institutional investors**: Investment trust management through Amove Asset Management + Sumitomo Mitsui Trust AM with 2  brand structure

### Partnership and M&A Strategy

- **Joint venture with SBI HD** ([[regional-banks/ssnb]] / SBI Sumitomo-Mitsui Net Bank): Major channel in online banking. Equity ratio should be confirmed in the latest disclosure with a reference date.
- **JV with Panasonic** (Sumitomo Mitsui Trust Panasonic Finance): Lease finance
- **Acquisition of former Nikko Asset Management** (2009  gradually from Citigroup, now Amove Asset Management): Strengthening investment trust management

### Medium-Term Management Plan "Trust 2030" (2024)

- Expanding pension, real estate, asset succession, and sustainability-related trusteeship centered on trust functions
- BaaS domain is indirectly deployed through SBI Sumitomo-Mitsui Net Bank ^[likely]
