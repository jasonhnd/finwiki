## TL;DR

Norinchukin Trust & Banking Co., Ltd. is a trust-bank operating company capitalized by [[cooperative-banks/norinchukin|Norinchukin]] 100%. **Holding both a banking license under the Banking Law and concurrent operations authorization for trust business under the Concurrent Operations Law**, it is a trust-concurrent financial institution that, within the Norinchukin Group—which serves as the central institution for JA Bank / the cooperative financial system—handles trust, asset management, and operations-related functions. Its distinguishing characteristic is that it is the sole trust bank affiliated with the central institution of cooperative-system finance.

## 2. Operating model

As a trust-concurrent financial institution, Norinchukin Trust & Banking carries out the following:

- **Trust receipt for the cooperative system**: Trust services for cooperative financial institutions, including trust receipt from credit unions (Shinren), etc.
- **Public interest and designated gift trusts**: Public interest trusts, designated gift trusts, and other civil trust domains.
- **Monetary debt securitization and real estate**: Securitization of monetary debts, syndicated loans, and intermediation of real estate trust beneficial rights.

While [[cooperative-banks/norinchukin|Norinchukin]] operates as the central institution of [[banking/cooperative-banking-japan|cooperative-system finance]] with a massive balance sheet, Norinchukin Trust & Banking is a separate legal entity handling its trust functions. It connects [[banking/ja-bank-system-japan|JA Bank System]] and [[banking/jf-marine-bank-system-japan|JF Marine Bank System]] to capital markets / trust products. Supervision is by the [[financial-regulators/fsa|FSA]].
