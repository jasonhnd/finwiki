### Three Functions of Toyota Group Finance ^[豊田通商・トヨタ自動車の有価証券報告書と各社公式 IR に基づく比較。EDINET: https://disclosure2.edinet-fsa.go.jp/]

| **Customer Sales Finance (auto-finance)** | [[leasing-firms/toyota-financial|TFS]] (holding) → [[card-issuers/toyota-finance|Toyota Finance]], etc. | Toyota vehicle auto-loans, dealer financing, credit cards ([[card-issuers/jcb|JCB]]-affiliated TS3 , etc.) |

This three-function system corresponds to functional division among **Toyota Motor Corporation (head office) / Toyota Tsusho (trading company) / each sales company (domestic / overseas dealer)**.

## 2. Business Segment Map ^[豊田通商 IR / 有価証券報告書。業務範囲は非上場子会社の開示制約があるため最新関係会社開示を優先: https://www.toyota-tsusho.com/ir/]

| Segment | Description |
|---|---|
| **CMS (Cash Management Service)** | Settlement concentration and fund pooling across Toyota Tsusho Group companies |
| **Bulk Factoring** | Accounts receivable purchasing / payment delegation across group companies (especially for small and medium suppliers related to the Toyota supply chain) |
| **Group Finance** | Short-term / mid-term loans to group companies |
| **Foreign Exchange and Interest Rate Hedging Intermediation** | Derivative intermediation for group companies (especially African and emerging market currencies) |
| **International Remittance Services** | Remittance and collection to overseas for group companies (including coverage of 54  African countries) |
| **Trade Finance Support** | Credit support linked to transactions in steel, automotive parts, manufacturing equipment, chemicals, and energy |

### Toyota Tsusho Business Structure and Finance Functions

- **Steel and parts logistics functions in the Toyota Motor Corporation supply chain**
- **African business** (former Toyota Motor Corporation's African sales subsidiary + 2012 年 acquisition of French CFAO covering all 54  African countries)
- **New areas such as renewable energy / lithium**

Toyota Tsusho Finance supports supplier finance / overseas currency hedging / cross-border remittance, etc. in these areas.

## 4. Regulation and Policy

- **Regulator**: Financial Services Agency (money lending / foreign exchange operations)
- **Regulatory Issues:**
  - **Transfer pricing tax** on intra-group financial transactions (especially transactions with Africa and emerging markets)
  - Boundary between group factoring and **Installment Sales Law / Money Lending Law**
  - **Elimination of internal transactions** in consolidated accounting
- **Recent Policy Issues:**
  - 2024~ [[financial-regulators/boj-monetary-policy|BOJ policy rate normalization]] → Re-evaluation of intra-group interest rates
  - Strengthening of AML/CFT for international remittances / cross-border fund transfers (with particular consideration for coverage of 54  African countries)
