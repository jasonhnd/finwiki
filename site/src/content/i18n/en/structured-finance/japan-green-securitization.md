# Green securitization in Japan — JHF Green MBS and evidence boundaries

## TL;DR

The publicly verified anchor in this review is JHF Green MBS. JHF's current materials expressly distinguish it from ordinary monthly MBS: Green MBS do not use overcollateralization, so their rating linkage differs. The previous page generalized from this product to unverified Japanese green RMBS, renewable-receivable ABS, EV ABS, securitization SLB overlays, Climate Bonds certification and a Japan/Europe “greenium” comparison. Those claims are removed unless a named transaction and framework document are public.

## 1. Label and structure are separate questions

[MOE's Green Bond Guidelines page](https://greenfinanceportal.env.go.jp/bond/guideline/guideline.html) and [ICMA's Green Bond Principles](https://www.icmagroup.org/sustainable-finance/the-principles-guidelines-and-handbooks/green-bond-principles-gbp/) support the following evidence checklist.

| Question | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| What is labeled green? | Named security and framework |
| What finances eligible assets? | Use-of-proceeds categories and selection process |
| How are proceeds managed? | Issuer framework and account process |
| What is reported? | Allocation and impact report, period and methodology |
| Who reviewed it? | Named external-review report and scope |
| What is the securitization structure? | Separate offering, trust and rating documents |

A green label does not establish credit enhancement, rating, investor base or pricing.

## 2. Verified JHF Green MBS boundary

[JHF's Green MBS plan](https://www.jhf.go.jp/about/investor/shisan_tanpo/green_mbs.html), [2026 MBS summary](https://www.jhf.go.jp/files/topics/5876_ext_99_0.pdf), and [FY2026 comparison](https://www.jhf.go.jp/files/topics/5007_ext_99_1.pdf) support the following table.

| Feature | Verified reading |
|---|---|
| Issuer | JHF |
| Product | Named Green MBS series backed under JHF's disclosed green framework |
| Bond / trust mechanism | JHF bond with the beneficiary-interest-event mechanics described in JHF documents |
| Overcollateralization | Green MBS are not structured with OC |
| Rating implication | Read the dated Green MBS rating; do not copy the ordinary monthly-MBS rating |
| Issuance data | Use the named Green series plan, presale and result |

## 3. Ordinary monthly MBS comparison

[JHF's FY2026 product comparison](https://www.jhf.go.jp/files/topics/5007_ext_99_1.pdf) supports the following narrow table.

| Field | Ordinary monthly JHF MBS | JHF Green MBS |
|---|---|---|
| Overcollateralization | Used for post-event beneficiary certificates | Not used |
| Rating | Dated ordinary-series rating | Dated Green-series rating |
| Eligibility | Flat 35 pool under ordinary product terms | Green-framework eligibility in the named documents |
| Evidence | Ordinary issue result and pool data | Green plan, presale / result and framework reporting |

This is not a senior/subordinated tranche comparison.

## 4. SLB and certification claims

[MOE's current guideline page](https://greenfinanceportal.env.go.jp/bond/guideline/guideline.html) supports the following rule for any claimed securitization SLB or certification.

| Claim | Minimum named evidence |
|---|---|
| Sustainability-linked terms | KPI, SPT, observation date, verification and financial consequence in the offering document |
| Coupon step-up | Exact contractual amount and period; no “typical 25–50bp” band |
| Climate Bonds certification | Named certificate, standard version and verifier |
| External review | Named report and scope |
| Japanese adoption | Defined transaction population and date |

No Japanese securitization SLB or Climate Bonds-certified SPV is asserted here without that package.

## 5. Cross-market comparison

[MOE's guideline page](https://greenfinanceportal.env.go.jp/bond/guideline/guideline.html) and a named foreign regime can support the following comparison method.

| Field | Comparison rule |
|---|---|
| Framework | Compare exact versions and legal / voluntary status |
| Product | Compare the same collateral and structure |
| Volume | Use defined period, currency and issuance measure |
| Pricing | Match date, currency, benchmark, life and credit |
| Verification | Name reviewer / verifier and assurance scope |

No generic Japan-vs-Europe scale, maturity or greenium hierarchy is retained.

## 6. Deferred claims

Japanese private green RMBS, renewable-receivable ABS, EV ABS, SLB overlays, certification adoption, investor demand and greenium remain deferred until supported by a public named transaction and direct link.
