# Japan trust bank vs global custodian comparison matrix

## TL;DR

This page compares what can be verified about Japanese trust banks and global custodians operating in Japan. It does not rank pricing power, infer customer mandates, or assume market connectivity from a group brand. Legal-entity status comes from FSA registers; business scope from each entity's official materials; infrastructure participation from the current operator lists; and scale metrics from dated disclosures with the issuer's definitions preserved.

## Master Trust vs Custody Bank of Japan — 2020 統合の意味

公式資料で確認できる事実は、**2020-07-27 に JTSB（日本トラスティ・サービス信託銀行）と TCSB（資産管理サービス信託銀行）が合併し、[[trust-banks/custody-bank|日本カストディ銀行]]となったこと**である。統合の競争効果や MTBJ との「2強」評価には、同一定義の市場全体データが必要であり、本ページでは測定済み事実として扱わない。^[Source: CBJ company information, https://www.custody.jp/corporate/information/index.html.]

## Research checklist

1. Confirm whether a custody-bank reference is **entity / shareholder nominee / mandate-holder** before drawing ownership conclusions, using [[banking/japan-trust-bank-custody-map|trust-bank custody map]].
2. Identify the exact legal entity and licence category in current FSA registers before describing its regulated role.
3. For global groups, distinguish the Japan branch, trust bank, securities company, and asset manager; do not combine their permissions.
4. Verify JASDEC, JSCC, BoJ, and ICSD connectivity from current participant or entity disclosures rather than group branding.
5. Source-date all AUC / AuA / 受託資産 figures because they move with market prices and new mandates; the Master Trust ¥823tn (2025) and CBJ ¥696tn (2025-03) anchors are official-disclosure summary points but not real-time numbers.
6. Cross-link client side via [[securities/japan-asset-manager-landscape-matrix|asset manager landscape matrix]] to understand mandate flows.
