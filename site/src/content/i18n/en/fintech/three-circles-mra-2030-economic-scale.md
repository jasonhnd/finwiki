# 2030 scenario analysis for three regulatory circles · estimates premised on no MRA

## Wiki route

This entry sits under [[fintech/INDEX|fintech index]]. Read it with [[fintech/japan-financial-regulation|Japan financial regulation: legal architecture for tokens, crypto-assets, and payments]] for adjacent context and [[fintech/japan-stablecoin-regulatory-landscape|Japan stablecoin legal architecture: the JPYC, USDC, and Project Pax three-layer model]] for the broader system boundary.

> [!info] TL;DR
> The **$130B** and **$384B** figures in this entry are scenario inputs for considering a future in which the US, EU, and Japanese regulatory circles become interconnected; they are not official forecasts or observed flows. The confirmed official baseline is that the GENIUS Act (S.1582) became law in the United States on 2025-07-18, MiCA applies in the EU, Japan operates its electronic payment instrument regime, and US and Japanese authorities have discussed digital assets and the need for internationally consistent supervisory frameworks. None of the official materials reviewed records a signed US-Japan, US-EU, or trilateral stablecoin mutual recognition agreement (MRA), an effective date, or automatic passporting. ^[GENIUS Act status: https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/1582; MiCA: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R1114; FSA-SEC dialogue (2026-02-27): https://www.fsa.go.jp/en/news/2026/20260227/20260227.html; FSA minister remarks (2026-04-17): https://www.fsa.go.jp/en/conference/minister/2026/20260417.html]

## Key facts

- **Scenario input A**: assume a 2030 regulated-circle balance of US $80B + EU $15B + Japan $35B = $130B. This is not an official forecast. ^[FinWiki scenario assumption; not an observed value or official forecast.]
- **Scenario input B**: the comparison value of $384B per year is also an assumption and must not be quoted as an observed country-level flow. ^[FinWiki scenario assumption; not an observed value or official forecast.]
- **Official baseline**: the GENIUS Act, MiCA, and Japan's electronic payment instrument regime exist as separate regimes, but the reviewed materials do not establish a trilateral MRA. ^[https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/1582; https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R1114; https://www.fsa.go.jp/en/policy/sftl/index.html]

## Mechanism / How it works

Scenario mechanism for segmenting a regulated circle and out-of-circle flows:

```
Regulated-circle scenario ($130B / 2030):  Out-of-circle flow hypothesis ($384B / year):
├── institutional custody (BUIDL/banks)   ├── sanctions evasion (Russia/Iran)
├── CME margin                            ├── retail remittance (Nigeria/Philippines)
├── bank RTGS interconnection             ├── grey-market merchant settlement
├── §501 chain-level denylist enforcement ├── USDT on Tron P2P
└── strong KYC / AML                      └── on-chain transfer without KYC

Segmentation logic: regulatory requirements + use cases + channel stickiness create a hard boundary
                    · expansion of the three circles does not automatically displace out-of-circle flows
```

**Separate observed facts from hypotheses**:
- The 2026-02 FSA-SEC dialogue covered crypto-assets, digital assets, and multilateral cooperation, but did not announce a signed stablecoin MRA. ^[https://www.fsa.go.jp/en/news/2026/20260227/20260227.html]
- The financial services minister's 2026-04 remarks said discussion of internationally consistent regulatory and supervisory frameworks was necessary; they did not announce an agreement. ^[https://www.fsa.go.jp/en/conference/minister/2026/20260417.html]
- Mutual recognition, passporting, and a 2027-2028 completion date remain future scenarios, not a confirmed policy timetable.

## Origin & evolution

The [[fintech/genius-act-501-denylist-mandate|GENIUS Act]] became Public Law 119-27 as S.1582 on 2025-07-18. It operates as a US regime separate from [[fintech/mica-overview|MiCA]] and Japan's electronic payment instrument framework. The $130B figure assumes that comparable regulatory determinations and interconnection develop in the future; the $80-100B downside case is also model output. Neither figure may be used as evidence that an MRA has been concluded or scheduled. ^[https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/1582; https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R1114; https://www.fsa.go.jp/en/policy/sftl/index.html]
