> [!info] TL;DR
> The market described as “tokenized MMFs” mixes US-registered MMFs, 1940 Act registered funds, private funds and tokenized products outside the United States. WTGXX is a registered money market fund, while BUIDL is a private fund that filed Form D; they are not in the same legal category. Circle announced its acquisition of Hashnote / USYC on 2025-01-21 but did not disclose the purchase price.

## Mechanism / How it works

Compare registration / private-placement grounds, investor eligibility, redemption, recordkeeping and underlying assets rather than just product names. The table below is based on [official WTGXX materials](https://www.wisdomtree.com/investments/etfs/fixed-income/wtgxx), [Circle's Hashnote acquisition announcement](https://www.circle.com/pressroom/circle-announces-acquisition-of-hashnote-and-usyc-tokenized-money-market-fund-alongside-strategic-partnership-with-global-trading-firm-drw), and the [BUIDL Form D](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2013810/000201439024000001/xslFormDX01/primary_doc.xml). Prioritize the latest offering documents for each product.

| Product | Operator | Route confirmed in public materials | Main access surface | Comparison note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BUIDL | BlackRock + Securitize | SEC Form D, Investment Company Act 3(c)(7) private placement | Eligible institutional investors | Not in the same category as a registered MMF |
| **WTGXX** | WisdomTree | 1940 Act registered Treasury MMF targeting a stable NAV | WisdomTree Prime / Connect | The fund does not guarantee principal and is not FDIC-insured |
| **USYC** | Hashnote / Circle | Tokenized money-market / Treasury product | Institutional and digital-asset markets | Circle announced the acquisition on 2025-01-21; price undisclosed |

WisdomTree Prime + WTGXX provides a route connecting registered fund shares with digital records and a wallet. Regarding the **distinctive role of Hashnote USYC**, Circle's 2025-01-21 announcement explains the acquisition of Hashnote / USYC and an intention to expand USYC as collateral in digital-asset markets. Do not fill in an undisclosed acquisition price or unannounced product integration.

USDC and USYC are legally and economically distinct products. Describing exchange or collateral use between them as a single product that “circumvents” an interest prohibition requires confirmation of current product terms and applicable law.
