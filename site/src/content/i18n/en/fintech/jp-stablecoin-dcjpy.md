> [!warning] Distinguishing the corporate entities
> The current operating company is **DCP Co., Ltd.** Its current corporate history records that DeCurret Holdings absorbed DeCurret DCP in 2026-07 and changed its name to DCP Co., Ltd. “DeCurret DCP Inc.” is retained only as the historical entity before 2026-07 in the corporate record. It is a different legal entity and business from the current S.BLOX, which succeeded to the former DeCurret's crypto-asset exchange business. See [[exchanges/jp-exchange-sblox|S.BLOX overview]] for details.

## How the platform works

DCP Co., Ltd.'s public service materials describe a design that connects a bank-side financial zone with a corporate-service-side business zone and links digital assets with DCJPY settlement. In analysis, record the following separately.

- The bank issuing the deposit token and the company operating the platform
- The status of a demonstration, technical validation, production validation or commercial service
- The entity responsible for issuance, transfer, redemption, identity verification and ledger records
- The target asset, settlement finality, incident response and participation eligibility

## Current status of the absorption merger and name change

The former company's [2026-04-02 announcement](https://www.decurret-dcp.com/pressrelease/pr-20260402.html) stated that the parent company planned to absorb DeCurret DCP Inc., with 2026-07-01 as the effective date. [DCP Co., Ltd.'s current company information and history](https://www.dcjpy.com/company/) subsequently record that the absorption merger and name change to DCP Co., Ltd. took place in 2026-07 in the corporate history. The current entity is therefore DCP Co., Ltd.; use the former name only as date-specific historical information.

## Due-diligence checklist

1. Confirm the issuing bank, deposit liability, eligibility and redemption terms in the project materials.
2. Record demonstrations, completed validations and commercial availability as different statuses.
3. For DvP or interbank settlement, confirm settlement finality, participants, operating hours and incident handling.
4. For a merger or corporate-name change, distinguish the planned announcement from post-effective-date public notice.
5. Do not automatically include DCJPY in CBDC, crypto-asset or general stablecoin-adoption counts.
