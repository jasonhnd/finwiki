## TL;DR

Dentsu Group announced the Dentsu Ventures Fund 2 CVC fund on 2021-04-27. The official release states a 100 億円 fund, global investment in domestic and overseas startups, investment from seed through later stages, and planned operation through 2031-03. ^[verified-2026-07-29]

## 2. Dentsu Ventures

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Business type | CVC (Corporate Venture Capital) |
| Investment targets | Domestic and overseas startup companies |
| Investment stage | Broadly from seed to later stage |
| Investment region | Global, domestic and overseas |
| Fund scale | Cumulative managed-fund total of about 200 億円 (Fund 1: 100 億円, formed 2015-04; Fund 2: 100 億円, formed 2021-04; Dentsu Group official) |

Sources: [Dentsu Ventures Fund 2 formation release](https://www.group.dentsu.com/jp/news/release/000439.html), which identifies the fund, its 100 億円 scale, global scope, and seed-to-later-stage remit.
