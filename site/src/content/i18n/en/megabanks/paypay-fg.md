## TL;DR

“PayPay Financial Group” is an **analytical label** used in this entry to examine links among payments, cards, banking, and securities; it does not mean that a statutory holding company bears that name. The core company, PayPay Corporation, listed on the Nasdaq Global Select Market as `PAYP` on 2026-03-12. After the listing, SoftBank and its group companies held 62.2% of the voting rights (40.8% economic interest), and PayPay remained a consolidated SoftBank subsidiary. SoftBank's FY2025 Financial segment reported JPY 404.5 billion in revenue and JPY 86.3 billion in segment income. PayPay disclosed 74 million registered users as of 2026-07. ^[PayPay IPO: https://about.paypay.ne.jp/en/pr/20260312/01/; SoftBank FY2025 results pp. 14-15: https://www.softbank.jp/en/corp/set/data/ir/documents/presentations/fy2025/investors/pdf/sbkk_investors_presentation_20260511_en.pdf; PayPay users: https://about.paypay.ne.jp/en/pr/20260702/01/]

## 1. Label and legal boundary

- **Legal entity**: PayPay Corporation
- **Founded**: 2018-06-15
- **Business**: development and provision of electronic payment services, including mobile payments
- **Headquarters**: YOTSUYA TOWER, 1-6-1 Yotsuya, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo
- **Registered location**: 1-3 Kioicho, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo
- **Market / ticker**: Nasdaq Global Select Market / `PAYP` (2026-03-12)

The company's founding date must not be confused with the payment service's launch date. PayPay's official company profile gives **2018-06-15** as the founding date; the former entry's “founded 2018-10-05” mixed it up with the timing of the service launch. ([PayPay company profile](https://about.paypay.ne.jp/en/about/))

PayPay's official profile lists PayPay Card, PayPay Bank, PayPay Securities, PayPay SC, PayPay India, and Credit Engine as group companies. SoftBank's segment disclosure, by contrast, reports voting-rights ratios on the SoftBank consolidated basis. Because these are not the same kind of “capital tree,” they are presented separately below.

## 2. Principal entities in SoftBank's Financial segment

| Entity | Voting-rights ratio disclosed by SoftBank | Main function | Disclosure note |
|---|---:|---|---|
| PayPay Corporation | 62.2% | Mobile payments and related services | Remained a SoftBank consolidated subsidiary after the Nasdaq listing |
| PayPay Card Corporation | 100.0% | Credit cards, card loans, and credit guarantees | Financial segment |
| PayPay Bank Corporation | 75.5% | Banking | Transferred from Media & EC to the Financial segment in FY2025 Q1 |
| PayPay Securities Corporation | 75.3% | Smartphone-focused securities services | Financial segment |
| SB Payment Service Corp. | 100.0% | Payment processing | Financial-segment entity separate from PayPay's official group-company list |

These are **SoftBank-side consolidated voting-rights ratios** from its year ended 2026-03 disclosure. They do not mean that PayPay Corporation directly owns each entity at the stated ratio. ([SoftBank, FY2025 results, p. 67](https://www.softbank.jp/en/corp/set/data/ir/documents/presentations/fy2025/investors/pdf/sbkk_investors_presentation_20260511_en.pdf))

| Metric | FY2025 / latest value | Definition / date |

| PayPay registered users | 74 million | Announcement dated 2026-07-02 |

| PayPay standalone number of payments | 9.2434 billion | FY2025 |
| PayPay consolidated GMV | JPY 19.4 trillion | FY2025; eligible payments of PayPay / Card / Bank after eliminating internal transactions |
| SoftBank Financial-segment revenue | JPY 404.5 billion | FY2025 |
| SoftBank Financial-segment income | JPY 86.3 billion | FY2025 |

GMV and number of payments do not use the same consolidation scope. SoftBank's data sheet defines GMV as a consolidated metric covering eligible payments of PayPay Corporation, PayPay Card, and PayPay Bank, while number of payments is a PayPay standalone metric. Dividing one by the other to derive a simple “average payment value” would therefore be invalid. ([SoftBank FY2025 earnings data sheet, pp. 15, 19](https://www.softbank.jp/corp/set/data/ir/documents/presentations/fy2025/results/pdf/sbkk_earnings_datasheet_pdf_20260511.pdf); [PayPay, 2026-03-18](https://about.paypay.ne.jp/en/pr/20260318/02/))

## 4. 2026 IPO

On 2026-03-11 US time, PayPay announced IPO pricing of $16 per ADS and said trading on Nasdaq was expected to begin the next day. The offering comprised 54,987,214 ADSs in total, split between newly issued shares from PayPay and shares sold by SVF II Piranha (DE) LLC. SoftBank's year-end materials confirmed completion of the listing and that PayPay remained its consolidated subsidiary afterward. The former entry's “IPO speculation” and “preparing to list” descriptions are therefore obsolete. ^[PayPay IPO pricing: https://about.paypay.ne.jp/en/pr/20260312/01/; SoftBank FY2025 results p. 15: https://www.softbank.jp/en/corp/set/data/ir/documents/presentations/fy2025/investors/pdf/sbkk_investors_presentation_20260511_en.pdf]

## 5. LINE Pay termination and LINE / PayPay integration

LINE Pay's Japanese service ended on 2025-04-30. This was not a transfer of the business to PayPay; users instead had a temporary option to transfer eligible LINE Pay balances to PayPay balances. LINE Points were not terminated and continued. ([LY Corporation, 2024-06-13](https://www.lycorp.co.jp/en/news/release/008632/))

On 2026-07-02, LINE Yahoo and PayPay announced LINE / PayPay account linking. Functions including PayPay payment, sending and receiving money, and mutual exchange of LINE Points and PayPay Points were to roll out in stages. The full set of functions had therefore not already been integrated in 2025. ([PayPay, 2026-07-02](https://about.paypay.ne.jp/en/pr/20260702/01/))

## 6. Functional map

| Layer | Entity | Ecosystem role |
|---|---|---|
| Wallet / payments | [[payment-firms/paypay|PayPay]] | High-frequency payment interface, identity verification, and sending / receiving money |
| Card | [[card-issuers/paypay-card|PayPay Card]] | Credit payments and post-pay flows inside the PayPay app |
| Bank | PayPay Bank | Deposits, account connection, PayPay Debit, and related functions |
| Securities | [[securities-firms/paypay-securities|PayPay Securities]] | Small-lot investing and in-app investment flows |
| Media / telecom interfaces | LY Corporation / SoftBank | Customer interfaces through LINE, Yahoo! JAPAN, and telecom subscriptions |

This is an analytical map of service flows, not the legal organization chart of one holding company. Each financial product has a separate entity, license, and customer-protection boundary under laws including the Banking Act, Financial Instruments and Exchange Act, Installment Sales Act, and Payment Services Act.

## 7. Disclosure cautions

- **Distinguish registered from active users**: registered users, identity-verified users, and monthly transaction users are different metrics.
- **Distinguish PayPay standalone from PayPay consolidated**: the consolidation scopes for payment count and GMV differ.
- **Distinguish the SoftBank segment from PayPay Corporation**: the Financial segment also includes entities such as SB Payment Service.
- **Distinguish voting rights from economic interest**: after PayPay's IPO, the SoftBank group held 62.2% of voting rights and a 40.8% economic interest.
- **Compare payment share on consistent definitions**: this entry does not use “approximately two-thirds of QR-code payments” as a KPI because the reviewed primary sources do not fix a comparable definition.
